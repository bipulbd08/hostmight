About Host Might

HostMight is a world-wide provider of Domain Registration , shared Hosting , Reseller Hosting , Managed VPS and Dedicated server. Privately held and based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. we've since grown to become one of the Bangladeshi fastest growing provider of Web Hosting solutions. the company was founded in 2010 by Md. Jobair Alam (Bipul).

History

In 2010, we entered the industry with the name " One IT BD " to make information technology easy for everyone to understand and use. In 2011, to build upon its vision of empowering businesses to leverage the latest technologies we re-entered the industry with new name " Host Might" bringing with new products, a fresh new approach to support, and more control for customers.
